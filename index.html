What it is

Your dash shows kilowatt-hours. Your bills arrive in dollars.

A plug-in hybrid runs on two fuels with two prices, and the car never joins them up. Efficiency screens show energy, range guesses drift, and nobody can tell you whether yesterday's commute cost two dollars or ten.

HybridEdge blends the costs, exactly when and how you used the energy. A small Bluetooth OBD reader plugs into the diagnostic port every car has, and the app reads the same live figures a workshop scan tool reads: battery level, fuel level, speed, odometer. It prices the electric kilometres with your actual electricity plan, the petrol kilometres with your actual receipts, and keeps the whole history on your phone.

Accuracy

Checked against your vehicle, not against a brochure.

The app's readings are validated against the car's own instruments, drive by drive, before they are shown as truth. When the app has not verified a number for your exact model yet, it says so and asks you to read the dash while it learns.

0.3%

How far the app's battery reading sat from the dash at full charge on the reference car, validated across the whole battery range.

±2%

Measured usable battery capacity agrees with the manufacturer's own cloud data to within two percent. The brochure figure was 10% off.

150+

While you drive, the app takes a fresh reading from the car more than 150 times a minute: battery level, fuel level, speed, and what the engine is doing. Every trip is built from thousands of real measurements.

Features

What it measures.

01 · True cost

Every trip in dollars, split by energy source.

Electric cost, petrol cost, and the combined per-100 km figure for each drive, each week, and each month. Compared honestly against what a petrol or diesel car would have cost.

Your figure comes from your own drives, not a brochure.

02 · Logbook

A logbook that holds up at tax time.

Every trip with odometer readings, places, and costs. Swipe to tag business or private, export a clean CSV for your accountant with the business-use percentage computed.

Record business kilometres. Give your accountant real data.

03 · Energy ledger

Every charge and every fill, one ledger.

Charges and refuels sit in one editable history, so the cost basis behind every trip is visible and correctable. Mistype a charge and you can fix it later without breaking the maths.

All your energy costs, logged and listed.

04 · Your real tariff

Charges priced by your actual plan.

Pick your electricity plan from nearly 200 real Australian tariffs. Free windows and time-of-use included. A midnight charge and a peak-hour charge cost different money, and the app knows.

Solar counts as free. Free windows count as free.

05 · Receipt scanning

Snap a refill, capture the cost.

Litres, price and total read straight off the receipt. Charging sessions scan too, from a screenshot of your charger's app or your charger's screen.

Every electron in your battery and litre of petrol in your tank has a price.

06 · PHEV database

Over 100 Australian PHEVs in the database, and counting.

Battery sizes, tank capacities, and consumption figures for the plug-in hybrids actually sold here, kept current without app updates. And multiple cars per household are supported.

Your model missing? It gets added on request.

07 · Live capture

Watch the drive as it happens.

Battery, fuel, speed, and which energy source is doing the work right now, live from the car while you drive. CarPlay included.

Measured values only. When the reader is off, the dials say so.

08 · Auto Drive

Start the car, and drive.

With a reader plugged in, Auto Drive starts the trip as you pull away and saves it a few minutes after you park, so a quick stop stays part of the same trip. It can be switched on once the app has learned your car's battery reading.

Nothing to tap at the start or the end.

09 · Charge detection

Home charging, logged for you.

With a reader plugged in, when the battery rises while the car is parked, the app records the charge: how much went in, when, and what it cost on your electricity plan. If it came from solar or a home battery, one tap says so.

No typing when you charge at home.

Privacy

Nothing to sign up to.

There is no account, and your trips, costs, and locations stay on your phone. Three things can leave it, all your choice: household sync, which copies a car's trips into your own iCloud for the phones you invite; anonymous calibration readings while your car's model is still being learned, including two numbers from each charge the app watches on cars that report their battery's power, which make the app accurate for every owner of that model; and a diagnostic report if you ever send one. The app asks you about calibration sharing plainly during setup. The privacy policy is short because there is so little to say.
