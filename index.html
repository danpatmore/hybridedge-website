What it is

HybridEdge answers the question the trip computer won't: what does each drive actually cost? Petrol and electricity together, per trip and per 100 km — with the split between them shown honestly, not averaged into a number nobody can confirm.

Log trips from the dash in ten seconds, or plug a $50 Bluetooth OBD reader into the port under the steering wheel and the car fills the numbers in itself — battery %, fuel level, odometer, second-by-second energy use.

Calibrated with photographs, not guesses

Development happens in a real PHEV, drive after validation drive, checking the app against the instrument cluster until they agree:

±0.3% battery gauge agreement with the dash, validated across the full charge window
Measured usable battery capacity from real trips — not the brochure figure
100% of costs traceable to a dash reading, a receipt, or a live decode

What it does

  • Live OBD trip capture — start from a notification, your Apple Watch, or the Lock Screen widget battery, fuel, odometer and speed recorded continuously; survives tunnels, carparks and coffee stops
  • Measured pure-EV kilometres per trip actual engine-off distance from telemetry — not an estimate from energy maths
  • True blended pricing for fuel and electricity half a tank of cheap fuel topped up with expensive fuel lands in between — the app does that maths on every litre and kWh
  • Fuel receipt scanning point the camera at the bowser; litres, price and total cross-checked to the cent before they're accepted
  • Regen and engine-charging, classified and shown separately see what those long descents actually put back
  • Insights computed from your own trips deterministic arithmetic over your data — no AI making numbers up

No account. No signup. No data collection.

The app tracks your car obsessively — the developer sees none of it. There is no login because there is nothing to log in to: your trips, routes and costs live on your phone, full stop. Read the privacy policy — it's short because there's so little to say.
